### Abstract
While LLMs generate convincing travel recommendations, they frequently violate hard budget, timing, transit, and opening-hour constraints. TRIP-PAL leverages LLMs for extracting user preferences and entity recognition while dispatching constraint satisfaction and temporal routing to symbolic automated planners, guaranteeing fully feasible and optimal travel itineraries.
