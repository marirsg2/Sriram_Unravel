### Abstract
Constructing Hierarchical Task Networks (HTNs) by hand is notoriously knowledge-intensive. We present Word2HTN, a novel framework leveraging distributional semantic models (word embeddings) over action descriptors to automatically induce method hierarchies from flat execution traces.
