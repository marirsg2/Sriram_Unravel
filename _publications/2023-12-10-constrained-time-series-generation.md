### Abstract
Generating synthetic time series satisfying strict physical, financial, and domain constraints is critical for real-world simulation and privacy-preserving data sharing. We formalize the constrained time-series generation problem and propose a novel architecture marrying score-based diffusion models with projected gradient methods to strictly satisfy non-convex constraint sets.
