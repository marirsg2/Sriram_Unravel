### Abstract
Evaluating the multi-hop logical deductions of LLMs requires rigorous ground-truth reasoning datasets. We present a programmatic framework to translate complex Boolean satisfiability (SAT) instances into natural language narratives with precise logical dependencies, creating un-gameable evaluation datasets for multi-step reasoning and deduction.
