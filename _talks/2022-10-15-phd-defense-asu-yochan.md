### Defense Summary
Presented comprehensive doctoral research investigating how cognitive limitations—specifically perceptual state aliasing, bounded mental modeling, and task-switching costs—can be mathematically formulated into automated planning and reinforcement learning algorithms to synthesize safe, intuitive, and human-aligned policies.
