### Presentation Details
Delivered an oral presentation at AAAI 2024 detailing how deterministic assumptions in existing algorithmic recourse frameworks lead to severe failure risks when humans attempt recommended actions in real-world stochastic environments. Introduced the SafeAR framework combining Markov Decision Processes with Conditional Value-at-Risk (CVaR).
