### Overview
When allocating complex interdependent tasks across human teams, ignoring context-switching mental fatigue and task duration variance leads to delayed deadlines and burnout.

- Developed decentralized heuristic scheduling algorithms that satisfy probabilistic makespans under execution uncertainty (ICAPS 2022).
- Designed an intuitive constraint-entry user interface allowing non-technical managers to define complex procedural rules without touching code.
- **Successfully deployed into production** at J.P. Morgan Chase to optimize critical operational processes.
