<h2>🎯 Core Research Focus</h2>

<div class="research-pillars-grid">

  <div class="pillar-card">
    <div class="pillar-header">
      <div class="pillar-icon"><i class="fa fa-random"></i></div>
      <h3>Sequential Decision Making</h3>
    </div>
    <p>
      Developing rigorous models and algorithms for multi-stage decision problems under uncertainty, stochastic execution, and temporal makespan constraints. Spanning <strong>Automated Planning & Scheduling (ICAPS)</strong>, <strong>Markov Decision Processes (MDPs)</strong>, <strong>Reinforcement Learning (RL)</strong>, and combinatorial optimization.
    </p>
    <div class="pillar-tags">
      <span class="tag">Automated Planning</span>
      <span class="tag">Reinforcement Learning</span>
      <span class="tag">Task Scheduling</span>
      <span class="tag">Stochastic Makespans</span>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="pillar-card">
    <div class="pillar-header">
      <div class="pillar-icon"><i class="fa fa-users"></i></div>
      <h3>Human-AI Interaction & Teaming</h3>
    </div>
    <p>
      Integrating insights from cognitive science and psychology into AI algorithms. Modeling <strong>human cognitive limitations</strong>, perceptual <em>state aliasing</em>, context/task-switching overheads, and intent predictability into policy synthesis, explainable AI (XAI), and <strong>safer algorithmic recourse (SafeAR)</strong>.
    </p>
    <div class="pillar-tags">
      <span class="tag">Human-Aware AI</span>
      <span class="tag">Explainable AI (XAI)</span>
      <span class="tag">Cognitive Modeling</span>
      <span class="tag">Safe Algorithmic Recourse</span>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="pillar-card">
    <div class="pillar-header">
      <div class="pillar-icon"><i class="fa fa-cogs"></i></div>
      <h3>Hybrid-Agent Systems (Generative + Classical AI)</h3>
    </div>
    <p>
      Bridging the linguistic fluency of <strong>Large Language Models (LLMs)</strong> and diffusion models with the strict correctness guarantees, world models, and search capabilities of <strong>symbolic automated planners</strong> and optimizers. Architecting reliable multi-agent systems, multi-hop reasoning synthetic data generation, and secure browser-based coding agents.
    </p>
    <div class="pillar-tags">
      <span class="tag">LLM + Symbolic Planning</span>
      <span class="tag">Reliable Multi-Agent Systems</span>
      <span class="tag">Multi-Hop Reasoning</span>
      <span class="tag">Synthetic Data Generation</span>
    </div>
